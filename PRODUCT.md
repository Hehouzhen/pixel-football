# Pixel Pitch

Browser football game with six players on the pitch per team, club seasons and MyCareer. Five domestic leagues each have 16 clubs and 30 home/away rounds, alongside a European cup. Stable player identities connect development, transfers, statistics and honors across seasons.

This update extends player abilities and event-based match simulation. A dedicated simulation page shows a changing scoreboard, important commentary, current statistics and player stamina. Pause allows tactical adjustments and substitutions; completed matches use the existing save, news and awards flows.

Preserve the existing English brand, dark navy/green visual language, football terminology and pixel match artwork. Simulation diagrams show abstract pitch zones and are distinct from the playable match sprites. Legacy saves and historical results must remain readable. Interface language is Chinese, with English brand labels where established.
